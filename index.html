Group Party Game

Poox

Bluff your way through weird prompts, then vote to pop the biggest fib.

How to play

  1. Choose 3+ players and pass one device around.
  2. Each round, one player becomes the Spotlight.
  3. The Spotlight secretly picks one truth and one bluff from a prompt.
  4. Everyone votes which statement is fake. Correct votes score points.
  5. First player to reach the target score wins!
